# Build costs: two-unit high-set building at 77 Foley Road, Woombye

Researched 2026-09-27. All dollar figures are AUD. Each figure gives its date and source. Where no primary source could be found, the figure is marked **estimate**. Budget arithmetic is shown so it can be re-run with quotes.

## Bottom line

- **$300,000 all-in will not build a ~110 m² two-unit high-set building on this site by any route that uses licensed builders.** The mid-case all-in cost is about **$590k–$630k**, or $5,300–$5,700/m². The low case, where most risks land well, is about **$410k–$480k**.
- **What $300k plausibly buys:**
  - **Conventional or kit plus builder:** about **45–75 m²** in total. That is one modest one-bedroom dwelling, or two very small studios at the optimistic end.
  - **Modular:** **one** installed one-bedroom module of about 30–45 m². A 54 m² two-bedroom module on this site is about $345k.
  - **Owner-builder:** about **60–95 m²**, but only for a single dwelling. See the next point.
- **The owner-builder route is probably not available for this design.** The Queensland Building and Construction Commission (QBCC) says owner-builders cannot "build or renovate multiple dwellings (e.g. duplexes, attached granny flats…)" ([QBCC](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/home-owner-hub/owner-build/about-owner-building), accessed 2026-09-27). Two self-contained halves sharing a wall is an attached granny flat or a duplex. Confirm with QBCC before relying on owner-building anything.
- **The realistic ways to fit $300k:**
  1. Build Stage 1 as **one ~55–65 m² dwelling**, designed so the second half can be added later against a prepared shared wall.
  2. Or increase the budget to **about $450k for the low case and $600k for a prudent case** for the full ~110 m².
- **Use the "dwelling house + secondary dwelling" approval path, not dual occupancy.** On a vacant residential lot the council infrastructure charge nets to $0 under the Sunshine Coast Council (SCC) charges resolution. Dual occupancy nets about $13k in 2021 base dollars, before indexation. Council application fees are also lower. The secondary half must stay within the council's floor-area cap: 60 m², or 90 m² in the Rural or Rural Residential zone ([SCC secondary dwelling information sheet, Sept 2023](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/~/media/Corporate/Documents/Planning/Planning%20Scheme%20help/Secondary%20dwelling%20information%20sheet.pdf)).

## 1. Cost per m² in South East Queensland (2025–2026)

### (a) Standard project home, single storey on a slab

- **ABS, Queensland new houses, FY2024-25:** average cost $458,868 over an average floor area of 230.9 m², or **$1,987/m²**. The national figure is $474,939 over 241.5 m², or $1,967/m². Queensland new townhouses averaged $375,361 over 167.8 m², or **$2,237/m²**. Source: [ABS Building Activity, Dec 2025, Average Cost and Average Floor Area data cubes](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/building-and-construction/building-activity-australia/dec-2025), released 2026-04-08.
  - Residential values include GST, include site preparation, and exclude land and landscaping ([ABS methodology, Sept 2025](https://www.abs.gov.au/methodologies/building-activity-australia-methodology/sep-2025)).
  - These are averages across large volume-built houses. They are a floor, not a quote for a small, complex building.
- **Escalation since then:**
  - ABS producer price index for house construction, Brisbane: **+8.0% for the year to the June quarter 2026**; +2.0% nationally for the quarter ([ABS PPI, June qtr 2026](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/producer-price-indexes-australia/latest-release)).
  - Cotality (formerly CoreLogic) Cordell index: Queensland **+1.0% in the June quarter 2026** ([Cotality, 2026-07-22](https://www.cotality.com/au/press-releases/construction-costs-rebound-to-steady-growth)).
  - Rider Levett Bucknall (RLB) forecasts Brisbane escalation of 5.0% in 2026 and 7.0% in 2027 ([RLB Riders Digest Queensland 2026](https://www.rlb.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2025/12/2026-RLB-Rider-Digest_Queensland_Digital.pdf)).

For example, a Coral Homes SEQ price list included only "up to $2,100 building approval and water connection fee allowance" and N2 wind rating ([Coral Homes price list](https://coralhomes.com.au/wp-content/uploads/Designer-Price-List-Brisbane-Gold-Coast-Sunshine-Coast.pdf), effective 2022-12-22, so stale).

### Infrastructure charges

Source: [SCC Infrastructure Charges Resolution (No. 9) 2022](https://assets-au-scc.kc-usercontent.com/330b87ea-148b-3ecf-9857-698f2086fe8d/d7778788-1fe6-45b8-aba2-197b606a66a2/925E6E7C-2BF9-43C6-A914-757D66CA8CA9), effective 2022-03-28. **Rates (Table 13.1):**

| Use | Charge |
|---|---|
| Dwelling house | **$30,677.65** per 3+ bedroom dwelling |
| Dual occupancy | **$21,912.60** per 1- or 2-bedroom dwelling unit; $30,677.65 per 3+ bedroom unit |

**How the charge is calculated:**
- **Vacant-lot credit:** a vacant residential lot gets a credit equal to Table 13.2 "Residential lot", **$30,677.65** (s 8.5).
- **Indexation:** base charges are indexed by the 3-yearly PPI average from a **March 2021 base date** (s 8.3), so 2026 amounts are higher than the table figures.
- **One notice covers Unitywater too:** the levied charge is split 54% to council and 46% to Unitywater for residential uses (Table 10.1).

**What that means for this lot:**
- **Dwelling house with a secondary dwelling:** one dwelling-house charge minus the lot credit is about **$0**. A secondary dwelling is part of a "Dwelling House" under the Planning Regulation 2017 ([SCC info sheet](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/~/media/Corporate/Documents/Planning/Planning%20Scheme%20help/Secondary%20dwelling%20information%20sheet.pdf)).
- **Dual occupancy (two 1–2 bed units):** 2 × $21,912.60 − $30,677.65 = **$13,147.55 before indexation**. My **estimate** is about $15k–$17k in 2026 dollars; ask council for written advice.
- **This assumes the lot is vacant with no prior charge history.** The README says no house has been placed.

- **Owner-builder:** about **60–95 m²**, only as a **single dwelling**. QBCC excludes attached granny flats and duplexes from owner-builder permits.

## 5. Cost-saving strategies for this plan

- **Choose the "dwelling house + secondary dwelling" pathway.**
  - It avoids dual-occupancy charges (about $13k+ base) and the $5,110 MCU fee.
  - It allows one water meter, one sewer connection, one driveway and one letterbox.
  - The secondary dwelling must be within 20 m of the main dwelling, share services and access, and stay within the floor-area cap: 60 m², or 90 m² in the Rural or Rural Residential zone ([SCC info sheet](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/~/media/Corporate/Documents/Planning/Planning%20Scheme%20help/Secondary%20dwelling%20information%20sheet.pdf)).
  - Since 2022, secondary dwellings can be occupied or rented by anyone, related or not (same source).
- **Put the wet areas back to back on the shared wall.** Place both kitchens, both bathrooms and a shared laundry on the separating wall so they share one plumbing riser, one drainage run down a post line, and one hot-water location. This shortens pipe runs and reduces SCC plumbing fixtures (**$110 each** in 2026/27).
- **Use one simple rectangle with a single skillion roof.** No valleys or hips, standard spans, and standard window sizes. Gutter on the high side away from the road only if needed.
- **Match standard module and transport widths.**
  - For modular, stay within Outhaus's **3.4 m standard-transport series**. The 4.0 m series needs a pilot escort ([Outhaus](https://outhaus.com.au/granny-flats/)).
  - For stick-built or kit, use 600 mm or 1,200 mm planning grids to suit steel frames and sheet sizes.
- **Keep the carport under and unenclosed** at the low end, using the high-set clearance. Use gravel hardstand first and seal later.
- **Stage the decks and stairs.** Build only the code-required landings and stairs in Stage 1, and add decks later. Decks in bushfire zones also attract BAL detailing.
- **Site the building to minimise BAL.** Within the clearing, maximise distance to classified vegetation to stay at BAL-12.5 or BAL-19 rather than BAL-29. The difference is about +3.8–4.3% vs +7.7% of build cost (Project BAL Build). Use non-combustible Colorbond and fibre-cement cladding, and limit glazing on the fire-facing side.
- **Suspend the floor over the overland flow path** on engineered steel posts or screw piles rather than cut, fill and retaining. This also helps the landslide overlay by minimising earthworks. Get the geotech early so post depths are priced correctly.
- **Use a gravel driveway first** (about $4k–$7k vs $10k–$19k for concrete). Concrete only the steep section and crossover where council requires it. Confirm the crossover standard ($680 permit).
- **Understand the owner-builder rules.**
  - A QBCC permit is required for work over **$11,000** incl GST. A QBCC-approved course is required first.
  - You get **one permit per 6 years**. The permit notification stays on the title for 7 years, and buyers within 6 years must be told.
  - There is **no home warranty cover**. Plumbing and electrical must use licensed trades.
  - Owner-builders **cannot build multiple dwellings, including attached granny flats** ([QBCC about owner-building](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/home-owner-hub/owner-build/about-owner-building); [apply](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/home-owner-hub/owner-build/apply-owner-builder-permit)).
  - The permit fee is **$493.59** as shown by QBCC on 2026-09-27 ([QBCC fees](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/home-owner-hub/owner-build/fees-owner-builders)). Courses are about $150–$300 (e.g. [ABE Education $179](https://www.abeeducation.edu.au/qld-owner-builder-course)).
  - Owner-builders *can* "complete a new home from a particular stage contracted to a QBCC-licenced builder". A single-dwelling Stage 1 could therefore be built to lock-up by a builder and fitted out by the owner.
- **Get fixed-price comparison quotes** from one modular supplier (Outhaus, Duncon or Able's Duplex range), one steel-frame kit/turnkey supplier (The Shed House or Wide Span plus a local builder), and one conventional sloping-site builder (e.g. Stroud Sunshine Coast). Give all three the same geotech and survey.
