## What the research found

**Approval path.** Build Stage 1 as one dwelling house with two wings: one full kitchen, one kitchenette, and one shared laundry. A house is accepted development in this zone if it meets the codes. That keeps the lot's single secondary dwelling for Stage 2, which needs no planning application at 60 m² gross floor area or less. Making Stage 1 two legal dwellings would use up both allowed dwellings, so Stage 2 would become a third ([planning-rules.md](planning-rules.md)).

**Budget.** $300,000 all-in will not build the full two-wing building of about 108 m² on this site. The estimate is about $410k–$480k in the low case and $590k–$630k in the mid case. $300k buys about 45–75 m² ([build-costs.md](build-costs.md)). Owner-building is probably not allowed for attached multiple dwellings, but is worth asking QBCC about if Stage 1 is approved as one dwelling house.

**Site plan** (measured in the site model). One layout fits everything together:

- Stage 1 starts at the front left of the clearing and runs down the slope, 10 m from the mapped waterway (Biodiversity overlay AO1.2) and 7 m from Foley Road.
- The floor sits at 31.0 m, level with the existing pad, so the front entry is step-free. The ground falls 3–4 m to the back, where two cars park underneath with 2.3–3.6 m headroom.
- The driveway continues from the pad down the right-hand side of Stage 1 and turns left under its back end. It is about 19 m long and drops 3.9 m (≈20%).
- Stage 2 fits in the pocket at the front right, beside the driveway: 4.8 × 6.2 m over two storeys, 60 m².

Layouts running parallel to Foley Road (the Twin Wing and Dogtrot concepts) block the driveway and the fence gate bay, and leave no room for Stage 2 with parking.

## The most cost-effective shape

Measured for about 108 m² on this site:

| Shape | Wall per m² of floor | Posts: total length / tallest | Parking area with 2.3 m headroom |
|---|---|---|---|
| Square, 10.4 × 10.4 m | 0.385 m | 33 m / 3.8 m | 40 m² |
| **Side by side, 9 × 12 m down the slope** | 0.389 m | 46 m / 4.1 m | 56 m² |
| Front and back, 8.4 × 13 m | 0.392 m | 48 m / 4.2 m | 62 m² |
| Twin Wing, 16.8 × 6.6 m along the road | 0.422 m | 27 m / 3.2 m | 18 m² |
| Starter half only, 4.5 × 12 m | 0.611 m | 39 m / 4.1 m | 36 m² |

- **The cheapest envelope is a compact rectangle close to square**, with one skillion roof, all wet rooms on one plumbing run, and the shared wall doubling as the structural spine. The 9 × 12 m side-by-side plan is within 1% of a square's wall-to-floor ratio. Its 4.5 m halves also span simply and suit standard module widths.
- **Down the slope costs more posts but pays for the carport.** Running the building along the road needs the shortest posts but leaves little headroom to park under, and blocks the driveway.
- **Floor area matters far more than shape.** Each square metre costs roughly $3,000–$4,100 before site costs. Taking 10 m² out saves more than any change of shape.
- **Building half now costs more per square metre**, because site works, stairs and services are paid up front. It is still the only Stage 1 option that fits $300k.

## Next checks

1. **Unitywater Service Advice Notice** ($469). The lot may sit outside the water and sewer connection areas. If so, it needs on-site wastewater treatment and rainwater tanks, which changes both cost and siting.
2. **Council pre-lodgement or written view** on a two-wing dwelling house with a kitchenette, and on the 10 m waterway buffer: whether the mapped line is a real bank.
3. **Contour and feature survey plus geotechnical report**, which confirm floor levels, post depths, and the parking headroom.
4. **BAL assessment**, which sets materials, and road-noise requirements for the north side.
5. **Quotes** for concepts 1 and 4 from a kit-home supplier with a builder, a modular supplier, and a local builder.
